### Week 1 Objectives:

* Connect with First Cloud AI Journey members and understand the internship rules.
* Learn cloud computing fundamentals, account management and basic AWS services.
* Become familiar with AWS Management Console, AWS CLI, Amazon EC2 and Amazon S3.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Week 1 Achievements:

* Understood cloud computing concepts and the importance of setting a billing alert early to monitor unexpected costs.
* Configured AWS CLI and used Linux commands and CloudShell alongside AWS Management Console.
* Practiced deploying infrastructure with VPC, EC2, S3 static website hosting, RDS and DynamoDB.
* Learned from practice that Linux command-line operations were more responsive than Windows Server on a low-specification EC2 instance.
